# Oxide Remote: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Oxide Remote ("Oxide") collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of its own.

## What stays on your devices

- **Your computers.** The names and addresses of the computers you add are
  stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync between your own
  devices through your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). The
  developer can't see them.
- **Passwords.** Passwords you choose to save are kept in your Keychain,
  synced through iCloud Keychain if you use it. The developer can't see
  them.
- **Settings and diagnostics.** Your settings, and the connection log
  shown under Settings → Diagnostics, stay on your device. Nothing is
  sent anywhere unless you copy or share it yourself.

## Connections

Oxide connects directly to the computers you add, running RustDesk, on
your network or over your VPN. The screen, keyboard and mouse input go
between your device and that computer only, never through a server run
by the developer. RustDesk's relay and ID servers are not used.

The connection is not encrypted. Use Oxide on a network you trust or
over a VPN, not over the open internet.

## The Demo Computer

The Demo Computer runs inside the app. Nothing you draw or type on it
leaves your device, and it is gone when the app quits.

## Children

Oxide collects no data from anyone, including children.
